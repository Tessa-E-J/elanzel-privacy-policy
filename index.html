Elanzel Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Welcome to Elanzel, a student productivity application designed to help users organise their studies, manage tasks, keep track of homework and calendars, and monitor their progress.

This Privacy Policy explains the information Elanzel handles, how it is used, and how you can contact us with privacy questions.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: Your email address and authentication information used to access your account.
  • Study information: Homework, tasks, marks, calendar entries and other information you choose to save.
  • Preferences: Settings and preferences you choose within the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used as necessary to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Save and display study information.
  • Provide Elanzel's productivity features.
  • Maintain security and support app functionality.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

Elanzel uses Supabase for authentication and data storage. Information needed to provide these services may be processed and stored through Supabase, subject to applicable service terms and privacy practices.

Information may also be processed when necessary to operate, secure or maintain the service, or when required by applicable law. The actual data-sharing practices must match the services and integrations used by the app.

4. Data Security

We aim to protect information using appropriate security measures. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their login credentials private.

5. Data Retention

Account and study information may be retained while an account is active or as necessary to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Information should be deleted or retained according to the applicable deletion procedures and legal requirements.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Elanzel's account-deletion feature is still being developed. If you have a question about your account or want to request assistance with deletion, contact our support email below.

Sending an email does not automatically delete an account or its associated data. Requests must be reviewed and handled according to applicable law and the deletion procedures actually available.

7. Children's Privacy

Elanzel is designed to support students. We aim to handle student information responsibly and comply with applicable privacy laws. Parents or guardians may contact us with questions about privacy or a child's information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Elanzel's features, data practices or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us
